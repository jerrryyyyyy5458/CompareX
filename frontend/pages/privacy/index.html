Your data, clearly explained

Privacy policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Our approach

CompareX is designed to make shopping comparisons easier without asking for more personal information than necessary. This policy explains what data we use and why.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address, optional name, and an encrypted password. We may also retain products you save, comparisons you create, and search queries to provide these features.

How we use it

We use account information to authenticate you and preserve your wishlist. Search and comparison activity helps deliver results and improve the service. We do not sell personal information.

Browser storage

Before you sign in, CompareX stores theme preference, wishlist references, and selected comparisons in your browser’s local storage. You can remove this data through your browser settings at any time.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information by emailing privacy@comparex.in. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@comparex.in.
